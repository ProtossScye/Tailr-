# 01 — Product Brief

## Problem hypothesis
Sports bettors encounter picks and slips across social posts, screenshots, bookmaker apps, and group chats. They may want help understanding or reshaping slips, plus a convenient way to record their own results. This is a hypothesis to validate—not a proven market fact.

## Initial audience
Start with Nigerian bettors who regularly see slips in social posts or Telegram groups, have market preferences, and want to review their own betting performance.

## Promise
Tailr helps users interpret and adapt slips to their preferences, then record bets they choose to place and review their results.

## Principles
- User autonomy: suggest and explain; don't silently make consequential changes.
- Personalization is editable and can be overridden per request.
- Distinguish AI suggestions, shared slips, user-reported bets, and independently verified records.
- Unknown data stays unknown until confirmed.
- Never request sportsbook credentials; collect only necessary information.
- No guaranteed-win claims or loss-chasing prompts.
- Web, Telegram, and X share one backend and preference profile.

## Positioning draft
“Tailr helps you make sense of betting slips, shape them around your preferences, and keep track of your own results—all in one place.”

## Validation and metrics
Interview 15–25 target users. Ask about real behavior and workarounds, not just whether they like the idea. Track onboarding completion, successful slip parsing, corrections, first bet recorded, weekly returning users, settled bets recorded, retention, and reported errors.
