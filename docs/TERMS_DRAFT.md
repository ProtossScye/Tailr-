# Draft terms and responsible-use note (NOT LEGAL TEXT)

> Written by an AI assistant as a starting point. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must not be published as the real terms until one has reviewed it for Nigerian law (data protection, consumer and gambling rules). Do not link it from the app yet.

## Draft terms of use (private beta)
1. Tailr is a bet tracker and slip assistant. It does not place bets, hold money, or give guaranteed predictions.
2. You use it at your own risk. Parsed slips and AI-read screenshots can be wrong; check every selection and number before you rely on it.
3. You must be of legal betting age where you live (18 or over in Nigeria; confirm with counsel) and may only use Tailr for your own records.
4. Do not upload other people's private information, or anything unlawful.
5. The beta may change, break, or end, and data may be lost. Export what matters.
6. You can delete your account any time from Profile. We may remove access for misuse.
7. Liability limits, governing law and dispute terms: to be written by counsel.

## Draft responsible-use note (could show on first sign-in)
- Betting can lose money. Only stake what you can afford to lose.
- Tailr shows your own results so you can see them clearly. It does not tell you what will win.
- If betting is causing you stress or harm, take a break and talk to someone you trust or a local support service.
